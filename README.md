# Shuncheng New Materials Co., Ltd.

Professional China manufacturer specializing in the production and global export of high-purity **Silicon Carbide (SiC)** materials.

### Core Products & Specifications
* **Green Silicon Carbide (SiC $\ge$ 99%)**: Microgrits (JIS #240 to #8000, FEPA F/P standards) for semiconductor slicing, precision optical polishing, and technical ceramics.
* **Black Silicon Carbide (SiC $\ge$ 98%)**: Coarse grits and powders for bonded & coated abrasives, sandblasting, and metallurgical deoxidizers.
* **Refractory Grade SiC**: Sintered grains and fine powders for high-temperature kiln furniture and wear-resistant castables.
